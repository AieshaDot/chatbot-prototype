ITSMF Chatbot Privacy Policy

Effective April 1, 2025 | Last updated April 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the ITSMF Chatbot (“Chatbot”), operated by ITSMF (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you interact with our Chatbot regarding:

  • How to become an ITSMF Member
  • Sponsorship Opportunities
  • Details About ITSMF Academies

By using our Chatbot, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you engage with our Chatbot, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Job Title & Industry
  • Membership interest
  • Sponsorship interest
  • Academy program interest

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address
  • Device type & browser info
  • Interaction timestamps
  • Referral source (social, website, direct)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide information on membership eligibility and application
  • Explain sponsorship opportunities
  • Share academy details
  • Send follow-up emails (with consent)
  • Improve chatbot accuracy and functionality

Automated Decision-Making

The chatbot provides automated responses. If you need further help, you can request to speak with an ITSMF representative.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Involvement

We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising purposes. - we have to make sure there is a way to safeguard this. However, we may share limited information with:

  • ITSMF Membership Team: If you express interest in becoming a member.
  • ITSMF Sponsorship Team: If you request details about sponsorship packages.
  • ITSMF Academy Admissions Team: If you inquire about academies and programs.

All ITSMF personnel and partners handling your data follow strict confidentiality and data protection protocols.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain chatbot interactions for [e.g., 6 months] to improve responses and provide follow-ups. After this period, data is either deleted or anonymized.

  • You may request deletion of your chatbot interactions at any time by contacting us at connect@itsmfleaders.org.
  • If you proceed with membership, sponsorship, or academy enrollment, your data will be governed by ITSMF’s Membership & Sponsorship Privacy Policies.

6. Security Measures

To protect your data, we implement:

  • Secure data transmission and storage.
  • Access Control: Restricted access to authorized ITSMF staff.
  • Regular Audits: Compliance checks and security monitoring.

Despite these safeguards, no system is 100% secure. In case of a data breach, we will notify affected users promptly.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights: Under GDPR (for EU users):

  • Right to access, modify, or delete your data.
  • Right to restrict data processing or object to automated decision-making.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.

Under CCPA (for California users):

  • Right to request details about data collection.
  • Right to opt-out of data sharing.
  • Right to request data deletion.

To exercise your rights, email us at connect@itsmfleaders.org.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our Chatbot may use cookies or tracking technologies to:

  • Improve user experience.
  • Analyze chatbot performance.
  • Provide relevant ITSMF membership, sponsorship, and academy information.

Users can adjust their cookie preferences via browser settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our Chatbot is intended for professionals and organizations and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If a child’s data is collected, we will delete it promptly.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes via:

  • ITSMF Website Announcements
  • Email (if applicable)
  • In-chat notifications

Please review this policy regularly for updates.
