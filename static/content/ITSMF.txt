<p>As the year draws to a close, we invite you to join us in making a lasting impact on the future of technology. The ITSMF Foundation is dedicated to providing college scholarships to underrepresented students pursuing technology-related degrees. Your generous contribution will directly support these talented individuals as they embark on their educational journeys.</p>
<p>Minority students receive 28% of all college scholarships according to College Stats.org. The Students for Fair Admissions vs Harvard case has caused many colleges and foundations in the face of increased pressure to change application criteria to eliminate race as a criterion. This means that students that are already underrepresented may continue to be. A 2022 report from the Pew Research Center reported that Black Students graduate with 9% of all STEM degrees.</p>
<p><strong>Why Your Support Matters:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bridging the Gap:</strong> ITSMF is committed to breaking down barriers that prevent first-generation college students, non-traditional students, and students from disadvantaged backgrounds from achieving their full potential. Your donation will help ensure that financial constraints do not hinder their academic aspirations.</li>
<li><strong>Nurturing Future Leaders:</strong> Our college mentoring program provides students with the support, guidance, and professional development opportunities they need to succeed. By investing in their future, you are contributing to the growth of a diverse and inclusive technology industry.</li>
<li><strong>Magnifying Excellence:</strong> ITSMF is dedicated to recognizing and celebrating the achievements of black technology students and executives. Your support helps us to foster a culture of excellence and inspire the next generation of leaders.</li>
</ul>
<p><em>Meet Rubena, a senior at Georgia State University majoring in computer information systems and cyber security and analytics. Rubena is a two-time recipient of ITSMF college scholarship program and participates in the college mentoring program.  Rubena has benefited from college scholarships, mentoring and lining up a job upon graduation. According to Rubena, The ITSMF Foundation scholarship was her first scholarship ever in undergrad. Ever since winning the first scholarship her confidence went up. Before receiving this scholarship, she would review the criteria and stop herself from applying. Rubena says, “This allowed me to begin to pursue every opportunity before me. The scholarship has helped me avoid debt. I probably would have dropped out due to debt. I got to study abroad in Morocco this summer. Thanks to ITSMF I can focus on my studies. I attended S1 and made 50 new contacts</em>.”</p>
<p><strong>We contributed a historic amount in scholarship awards and stipends at the Technology Leader Awards Gala this year, $100,000. We want the 2025 TLA to be just as impactful if not more. Every dollar we receive makes a difference.</strong> Your contribution will directly fund scholarships, mentoring programs, and other initiatives that empower underrepresented students to pursue their dreams in technology.</p>
<p>Thank you for your continued support of the ITSMF Foundation. Together, we can create a brighter future for the next generation of black technology leaders.</p>

<p><strong>The Future of Tech is Bright: The Power of Internships</strong></p>
<p>As a CEO, I have always believed in the power of mentorship and providing opportunities for the next generation. This summer, ITSMF had the privilege of hosting two incredibly talented interns. Interns were paid through the ITSMF Foundation. The Interns learned and gained work experience this summer. Their contributions to our organization have not only been invaluable but have also served as a powerful reminder of the bright future that lies ahead for Black executives in tech.</p>
<p>Our interns, both majoring in technology, were tasked with completing challenging capstone projects that required a combination of strong project management, organizational skills, research, and data analytics. They exceeded our expectations in every way, delivering exceptional presentations that showcased their professionalism, intelligence, and potential.</p>
<p>One intern analyzed multiple years of membership and Global Institute for Professional Development survey data, identifying key trends and insights that will inform our future strategies. The other intern conducted research on Black Tech Founders with over $1 million in revenue, providing valuable insights into their businesses and potential collaborations through our Ignite program.</p>
<p>Witnessing these presentations, I was struck by the immense talent and potential of these young individuals. Their passion for ITSMF, coupled with their strong work ethic and analytical skills, made them invaluable assets to this organization.</p>
<p>By providing opportunities for students to get to and through college, and by supporting their growth and development in the workforce, we are not only investing in their futures but also ensuring a brighter future for the tech industry as a whole.</p>
<p>I am proud to lead an organization that attracts such exceptional talent and is committed to fostering diversity and inclusion. The work that our interns have done this summer will have a lasting impact on ITSMF, and I am confident that they will go on to achieve great things in their careers.</p>
<p>A study by the National Association of Colleges and Employers found that college graduates with internship experience earn more than those without. So, there are short and long term benefits for interns.</p>
<p>The future of Black executives in tech is bright, and it is up to us to continue providing opportunities and support for the next generation of leaders. Let us celebrate the incredible talent of our interns and inspire others to follow in their footsteps.</p>
<p>ITSMF Intern Jordan Tolliver just graduated from High School and plans to attend University of Pennsylvania in the fall. ITSMF Intern Maxxim Jean-Baptiste is a Senior at the State University of New York at Buffalo.</p>

<p><div class="et_pb_section et_pb_section_8 et_section_regular" >
				
				
				
				
				
				
				<div class="et_pb_row et_pb_row_8 et_pb_equal_columns et_pb_gutters3">

				<div class="et_pb_text_inner"><p>Greetings, Family!</p>
<p><strong>Our New Reality: Fear and Innovation Cannot Exist Together</strong></p>
<p>As artificial intelligence rapidly evolves, organizations are grappling with how to harness its potential while simultaneously nurturing human capital. While technology advances at an unprecedented pace, we are still struggling to fully realize the potential of our workforce.</p>
<p>Harvard Business School Professor Amy Edmondson, a leading expert in psychological safety, underscores a critical challenge: even the smartest, most motivated employees may withhold their valuable contributions due to fear of criticism or rejection. This phenomenon hinders innovation, problem-solving, and overall organizational performance.</p>
<p>The modern workplace is increasingly characterized by collaboration and cross-functional teams. While this trend holds promise for enhanced productivity and decision-making, our research indicates a disconnect. Despite the surge in collaborative activities, psychological safety, the bedrock of effective teamwork, often lags behind.</p>
<p>Psychological safety is the cornerstone of high-performing organizations. It is the assurance that employees can contribute openly, challenge the status quo, and learn from mistakes without fear of retribution. This environment fosters innovation, creativity, and employee engagement.</p>
<p>Consider this: an employee witnesses a significant workplace challenge but chooses to remain silent due to fear of repercussions. This scenario, unfortunately, is more common than we might think. It highlights the urgent need to cultivate cultures where employees feel empowered to speak up and contribute to their full potential.</p>
<p>The confluence of factors such as remote work, rapid technological change, economic uncertainty, and organizational restructuring can erode employee trust and morale. To thrive in this complex landscape, leaders must prioritize the creation of psychologically safe environments.</p>
<p>By investing in psychological safety, organizations can unlock the full potential of their workforce, drive innovation, and achieve sustained success.</p>
<p><strong>I encourage you to read <em>The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace for Learning, Innovation, and Growth</em> by Amy C. Edmondson.</strong></p>
<p>According to Harvard Business Review, as business becomes increasingly global and cross-functional, silos are breaking down, connectivity is increasing, and teamwork is seen as a key to organizational success. According to data we have collected over the past two decades, the time spent by managers and employees on collaborative activities has ballooned by 50% or more.</p>
<p>If teams in your organization are spending more time collaborating and you want to ensure that your culture supports psychological safety, open communication, and vulnerability to ensure maximum performance, please contact Michael Whitley at <a rel="noreferrer">Michael.whitley@itsmfleaders.org</a> or Cedric Cobb at <a rel="noreferrer">Cedric.cobb@itsmfleaders.org</a> at ITSMF to learn more about the Cultural Impact Workshops that ITSMF delivers inside organizations.</p>

<p><strong>Why This Downturn is Your Opportunity to Lead on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion</strong></p>
<p><strong>Tech leaders, are you ready to disrupt the status quo again?</strong></p>
<p>This month, we witnessed a historic milestone: 157 graduates from our Global Institute for Professional Development, now equipped to propel their organizations forward with cutting-edge tools, renewed energy, and a powerful network. But let&#8217;s be honest, the headlines are filled with negativity – global instability, hiring slowdowns, and economic uncertainty. It is enough to make anyone wonder: will diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives fall by the wayside?</p>
<p><strong>My answer? The names of many corporate programs and initiatives may change, some corporate titles may change, some organizations may find reason to abandon their commitments but as a whole, Absolutely not.</strong> In fact, these very challenges present a unique opportunity for tech leaders to <strong>champion DEI</strong> and unlock its full potential. Here is why:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>A Multigenerational Workforce:</strong> For the first time ever, we have five generations working side-by-side. Leaders who can bridge these divides and harness the unique strengths of each generation will be the ones who thrive.</li>
<li><strong>The War for Talent:</strong> The post-pandemic workforce prioritizes flexibility, well-being, and a healthy work-life balance. To attract and retain top talent, you need to offer a workplace that reflects those values.</li>
<li><strong>The ROI of Well-being:</strong> A stressed workforce costs companies billions. By fostering psychological safety, where employees feel comfortable taking risks and sharing ideas, you can unlock innovation and boost productivity.</li>
<li><strong>DEI Programs are Showing Impact </strong><strong>Companies in the top quartile for diversity are 36% more likely to have financial outperformance</strong> compared to their less diverse counterparts.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The statistics are clear:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>77% of workers</strong> experience work-related stress (American Psychological Association).</li>
<li>Workplace stress costs the US <strong>$190-$300 billion annually</strong> (American Institute of Stress).</li>
<li>Only <strong>26% of leaders</strong> create psychologically safe environments (McKinsey &amp; Company).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Here&#8217;s the bottom line:</strong> DEI isn&#8217;t just the right thing to do, it&#8217;s a strategic imperative for success in today&#8217;s competitive landscape. By investing in your people, creating a culture of psychological safety, and embracing a multigenerational workforce, you will be well-positioned to weather any storm and emerge even stronger.</p>
<p><strong>Join us</strong> in building the future of tech leadership.</p>
<p><strong>This is not just about riding out a downturn; it is about seizing the opportunity to lead. Are you in?</strong></p>
